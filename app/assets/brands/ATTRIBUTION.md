# Collection brand marks

Local SVG files identify the services and studios named in the catalog. Letter
and symbol paths are retained; view boxes are tightly cropped to their actual
painted bounds, without extra padding. Marks
have transparent backgrounds. Marvel's rectangular backing is removed and its
lettering uses the original red. Apple TV and HBO are white on dark cards and
black on focused white cards; Hulu uses green, darkened on focused white cards.

- Netflix: https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/main/logos/netflix.svg
- Apple TV: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/appletv.svg
- HBO: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/hbo.svg
- Hulu: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/dc63ec43dfef33f81054e1d472270124f1ca40a5/icons/hulu.svg
- Marvel Comics: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marvel_Logo.svg
- DC Comics: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DC_Comics_logo.svg

Simple Icons source license: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md

Artwork is bundled with the app; no logo requests go to these sites at runtime.
